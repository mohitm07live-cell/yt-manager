Mohit M07 Official Manager

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction & YouTube API Services

Mohit M07 Official Manager ("we", "our", or "the application") is a creator management and live broadcast assistant tool designed to automate live chat moderation, chat commands, live alerts, and audience engagement for YouTube live streamers.

Mohit M07 Official Manager uses the YouTube API Services to provide real-time chat monitoring, alert triggers (subscribers, Super Chats, memberships), and stream statistics. By using this application, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Google Privacy Policy

This application complies fully with Google API Services User Data Policy. We encourage users to review the Google Privacy Policy to understand how Google processes and protects personal information.

3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

When you authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0, our application accesses:

  • Channel Profile Information: Channel name, channel ID, and thumbnail to display your identity on your local dashboard.
  • Active Live Broadcasts: Broadcast ID and live chat ID of currently active streams to monitor live chats.
  • Live Chat Messages: Real-time chat messages to trigger automated bot commands (e.g., !uptime, !discord), spam moderation, and sound/TTS alerts.

Zero Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, lease, or distribute user data, chat transcripts, or channel information to any third parties or advertising networks.

4. Data Storage and Retention Policy

  • No Cloud Storage: All access tokens and bot configuration settings are stored strictly in your local instance/browser session.
  • Live Stream Data: Live chat messages are processed in-memory in real time for bot replies and alerts; they are not permanently stored on external servers.

5. Revocation of Access & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your authorization. You can revoke this application's access to your YouTube and Google account at any time by visiting the:

Google Security Settings Permissions Page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Upon revoking access, the application immediately loses all ability to interact with your channel or stream data.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or terms of use for Mohit M07 Official Manager, please reach out via your creator management dashboard.
